What it does

Everything your build deserves

From oil changes to full engine swaps — GarageOS keeps the complete history of every vehicle you own.

🔧

Service Records

Log every maintenance event with date, mileage, parts, and cost. Never wonder when you last changed the oil.

📖

Build Journal

Document each build phase with notes, photos, and milestones. Share your build narrative publicly.

🧾

Receipt OCR

Snap a photo of any receipt — GarageOS reads it, extracts the parts, and logs the cost automatically.

💰

Cost Dashboard

See exactly what you've spent on each vehicle, by category, over time. No spreadsheet required.

⚡

Dyno Log

Record power runs and track how your tune evolves with every modification.

🔔

Reminders

Set mileage or calendar-based reminders for service intervals so nothing falls through the cracks.

🗂️

Mod & Parts Provenance

Track where every part came from, what it replaced, and why you installed it.

🚗

Multi-Vehicle

Not VIN-locked. Manage your truck, daily, and project car all in one place.
